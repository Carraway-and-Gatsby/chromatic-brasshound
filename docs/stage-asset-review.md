# Stage asset review

Current verified cartridge: `940e6b886e5576c5aaa2910192939e9b471b5705ba6a71cd3978657540265d63`.
All three missions have three authored sections and a separate boss arena.
The twelve landmark frames were reached using ordinary campaign inputs and
zero stage, position, health or inventory memory writes.

## CINDER

![Cinder sections and IRONMAW](screens/cinder.png)

Disconnected skyline shapes were replaced with connected factory roofs and
chimneys. Near walls use quieter metal shading. IRONMAW remains a low crawler
with a recessed cannon and furnace weak point.

## TIDEBREAK

![Tidebreak sections and DREDGER](screens/tidebreak.png)

Container corrugation distinguishes cargo from city masonry. Gap water is
recessed so it does not read as a platform edge. DREDGER uses a gantry, hoist
claw, pump vessel and stabilizers.

## BLACKOUT

![Blackout sections and NULLCORE](screens/blackout.png)

Larger bolted panels and dimmer pipes reduce visual noise behind gameplay.
NULLCORE uses a tall containment column, terminal arm and exposed red crystal.

Shared darkest BG ink avoids opaque padding rectangles. Weapon supplies are
metal field cases with distinct rifle/missile stencils; medical supplies have
cyan crosses. ROOK's brass/charcoal16x24 poses were checked across these palettes.
All frames are actual160x144 PPU captures, enlarged2x with nearest-neighbor.
See the [campaign report](verification/campaign/report.json) and
[ROOK review](rook-review.md) for evidence and remaining limits.
